# SCR-02 · Find and verify scripture passages

**Owner** · Pending confirmation · [Checklist](checklist.json) · [Guide](https://github.com/aispanda/Reusable-Delivery/blob/45a5e6653f202e37f181dce8c14e6bc0d34278b7/migrate/DELIVERY.md)

**Outcome** · As a scripture researcher, I want to find the intended passage and relevant commentary by citation, wording or meaning so that I can verify the source and understand its context.

**Scope** · A local search MVP for a classical Hindu pilot, starting with the Vijñāna Bhairava and its available editions and commentary. Reuse the Legal-AI portable importer and Tantivy baseline. The comprehensive catalogue and its source reconciliation take priority. Markdown is the sole corpus text format. The wider source catalogue covers paths to Vedas, Upanishads, Tantras, Āgamas, other Hindu and Buddhist sources, and works of spiritual masters. Broader indexed coverage and generated answers are later increments. [SCR-01 catalogue](../story.md)

**Operations** · Convert eligible, catalogue-backed sources to UTF-8 Markdown. Keep provenance and original evidence separately. Record source URL, edition, rights, source and Markdown checksums, conversion method, locator mapping and review status. Upload eligible Markdown to the private corpus bucket and record each object after readback. Export reviewed pilot passages to Legal-AI JSONL and return ranked results with original wording, edition and locator, surrounding context and attributed commentary where an alignment is reviewed. Report insufficient evidence where applicable. [Legal-AI architecture](https://github.com/aispanda/Legal-AI/blob/main/docs/architecture.md)

**Acceptance** · Pending human decision. Formal results for A-01 through A-06 are NOT RUN.
